Photoso Support

Need help? Email photoso@vaibhavgupta.co.in, or open a support request (a free GitHub account is required for GitHub). Include your device model, iOS version, and a short description of the problem. Please do not attach personal photos.

Browse your gallery

Open Home to browse your Apple Photos library. Tap a photo or video to open it full screen. Swipe left or right to move between items, and swipe down to return to the library. Your photos remain in Apple Photos; Photoso does not move them into a separate storage service.

What are Pockets?

Pockets bring related shots together in your gallery. Tap a Pocket to see its photos, then tap a photo to open it. Pockets change how your library is displayed; they do not delete, duplicate, or move your original photos. Pro similar-photo group review is a separate cleanup feature.

Explore Photo Map

Open Photo Map from the map button in the Home library header. It shows photos with saved location information. Photos without a location will not appear as a place on the map. A place name or map may need an internet connection to Apple's services.

Does the free swipe limit stop gallery browsing?

No. The daily allowance applies to swipe cleanup. You can still browse your library and open the Review Bin after reaching it. Photoso Pro adds unlimited swipes and other optional features; it is not required just to browse your gallery.

Photoso can't see my photos

Open the iOS Settings app → Apps → Photoso → Photos, and choose Full Access. If you chose Limited Access, Photoso can only show the photos you selected; you can add more from the same screen.

Where do deleted photos go?

Photoso never deletes anything until you confirm it in the review step. Confirmed deletions are performed by Apple Photos and move items to the Recently Deleted album in the Photos app, where they can be recovered for up to 30 days. If iCloud Photos is turned on, deletions also apply to your other devices that use the same Apple Account.

Photoso is not a backup service. Keep a separate backup of photos you can't replace.

Restore purchases

Open Photoso → Settings and tap Restore Purchases while signed in with the Apple Account you used to buy Photoso Pro.

Manage or cancel a subscription

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Open your App Store subscriptions or iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions. For refunds, use reportaproblem.apple.com.
